# Ameyalli Yetlanezi

Ameyalli is a research-only repository about the morphology, documentary
history, and modern use of the personal name **Ameyalli Yetlanezi**. The public
study is [`THESIS.md`](THESIS.md), written in Spanish because the question,
primary-source tradition, and intended readership are Spanish and Nahuatl
facing.

## Finding in one paragraph

The two lexical elements are documented in colonial Central Mexican Nahuatl.
*Āmēyalli* denotes a spring or source of water, while *ye tlanēci* means that
dawn is already arriving. The evidence does not establish the pair as a
pre-Hispanic personal name or as one fixed Nahuatl compound. The documented
personal-name pairing is modern, while the lexical material is much older.

## Epistemic boundary

Morphology and lexicography can establish documented forms, grammatical
structure, and historical glosses. They cannot recover every social,
pragmatic, or emotional value that a community attached to a word. Part I of
the study defines that boundary before Part II presents the philological
analysis. Readers should take claim strength from the evidence labels, not
from the appearance of academic formatting.

| --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | Attested | Direct source or reviewed descriptive authority |
| **B** | Inferred | Explicit grammatical or historical reasoning |
| **C** | Speculative | Unverified proposal or documented folk meaning |

## Key conclusions

- *Ameyalli* is securely documented as a spring or source of water.
- *Ye tlanēci* is an impersonal clause meaning that dawn is arriving.
- The fused lemma *yetlaneci* is present in Molina's 1571 vocabulary.
- The two-name sequence is not treated as one fixed Nahuatl compound.
- No consulted pre-twentieth-century source establishes the pair as a personal
  name; this remains a bounded negative finding, not proof of impossibility.
- Mexico City civil-registry data documents modern use but does not establish
  national frequency.

## Language policy

`THESIS.md` remains Spanish. Repository governance, methodology, provenance,
source records, integration contracts, and contributor instructions are
English. Nahuatl forms retain the orthography required by the research
context. Automated editing must not translate the study or flatten its
document-language and object-language distinction.

## Research assistance

ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Cursor, Model Context Protocol tools, Firefox, ripgrep,

`docs/bibliography/tools/`. None of those systems is factual authority. A claim
enters the study only through retained source evidence and explicit human
review.

## Reproducibility

Git history is the durable record of accepted changes. Source identities and
verification limits live in individual bibliography and provenance records.
Jig validates repository structure and machine contracts; it does not certify
historical or linguistic truth. GitHub Actions repeats that validation on a
Linux host using immutable Jig source plus the reviewed compatibility patch
recorded under `.github/patches/`.

## Scope and limitations

This is not peer-reviewed scholarship, an official linguistic ruling, or an
institutional statement. It is a falsifiable independent study prepared from
public sources. Several facsimile and demographic questions remain open and
are listed without pretending that formatting closes them.
