# Evidence methodology and source hierarchy

## Status

Active.

## Purpose

Define how `THESIS.md` distinguishes documented facts, explicit inference, and
speculation. This method constrains the article; it does not expand its
conclusions.

## Evidence labels

- **A — Attested:** present in a primary source, directly measured dataset, or
  reviewed descriptive authority appropriate to the claim.
- **B — Inferred:** follows from an explicit grammatical, historical, or
  comparative argument without a direct occurrence of the final claim.
- **C — Speculative:** lacks adequate documentary support, including folk
  etymology and irregular reanalysis.

One form may receive different labels at different analytical levels. A
morpheme may be attested while its proposed use as a personal name remains
speculative. The label follows the claim, not the spelling.

## Source hierarchy

1. Colonial primary sources establish historical forms and recorded glosses.
1. Academic grammar and lexicography analyze structure and comparison.
1. Reviewed ethnohistorical and sociological work supports modern movement
   history.
1. Institutional datasets support bounded contemporary measurements.
1. Search tools and AI assistants support discovery only.

A primary source is strongest for what it records, not automatically for every
analysis attached to that record. Modern scholarship may correct segmentation
without erasing the historical occurrence.

## Negative claims

Failure to find the paired personal name before the twentieth century is a
bounded search result, not proof of global absence. Every negative claim must
name the searched corpus, date, region, and access limitation. One valid
counterexample is sufficient to reopen the conclusion.

## Verification

Claim-level verification records exact editions, entries, folios, pages,
datasets, and known access limits. Individual source records live under
`docs/bibliography/sources/`; the cross-source status table lives in
`docs/bibliography/provenance/verification-status.md`.

## AI assistance

AI systems may propose searches, compare hypotheses, or draft text. Their
outputs do not count as evidence and must be checked against retained sources.
The governing decision is `docs/research/adr/ai-assisted-research.md`.

## Privacy

Contemporary name use is described through aggregates and source categories.
The study excludes dossiers of private people and any material that could
unnecessarily identify a minor.
