# Research

## Purpose

Index the research question, method, decisions, and bounded conclusions.

## Owns

Research design, evidence labels, claim strength, privacy boundaries, and study
navigation.

## Does not own

Tool configuration, hosted-platform behavior, or external-source identity.

## Contents

- Root `THESIS.md` is the public Spanish study.
- `methodology/evidence-methodology.md` defines evidence classification.
- `adr/editorial-conventions.md` governs language and composition.
- `adr/ai-assisted-research.md` governs automated research assistance.
- `papers/README.md` indexes the public output and citation metadata.
