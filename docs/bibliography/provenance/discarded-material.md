# Discarded material

## Status

Retained exclusion record.

## Private-person dossiers

Early research reports assembled names, professions, and institutional links
from dispersed public records. The material was removed because the argument
requires aggregate use, not dossiers about private people. At least one record
could concern a minor, making exclusion mandatory.

## Unsupported attribution

Claims were removed when a named author could not be verified as making the
specific statement attributed to them. General grammatical facts may remain
when supported by admitted descriptive literature, but invented precision is
not evidence.

## Unrecoverable archaeological claim

A claim about a carved glyph in an Amealco church mixed distinct testimonies
and could not be recovered from the located sources. The article now retains
only the Códice de Huichapan evidence recorded by Brambila Paz.

## Decorative references

Works without a claim-level citation were removed rather than retained for the
appearance of scholarship. A source may return only with a concrete repository
use and an individual record.
